# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, professional, harassment-free, and inclusive
community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience level,
nationality, appearance, disability, or personal beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate in technical discussion.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, licensing, access restrictions, and security embargoes.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Prioritize the health of the community over individual advantage.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, publishing
private correspondence, or encouraging others to engage in such conduct.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and project events, as well as when someone publicly represents the project.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict interaction,
or permanently ban participation when behavior violates this policy. Decisions
should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid retaliation against a
good-faith reporter.

Report conduct concerns privately through the same GitHub private reporting
channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), with “Conduct report” in the
title. Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent possible.

This policy is inspired by established open-source community codes and is
provided under the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
